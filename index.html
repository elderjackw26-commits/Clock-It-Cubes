How to use Clock It

1. Scramble your cube and choose an inspection time.

2. Hold both Shift keys together, then release to begin inspection. Choose “None” to skip inspection and start solving on this release.

3. During inspection, hold both Shift keys again and release to start solving.

4. Press Space to stop and save the solve. Esc resets.

The +2 inspection penalty is on by default. You can also choose to cancel an attempt if the second hold is more than two seconds late.
